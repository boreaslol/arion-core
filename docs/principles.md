# Principles

## Core Proposition

> **Reality provides experience; counterfactuals produce agency.**

Experience records the path that occurred. Agency begins when a system asks
what else could have happened, what would distinguish competing explanations,
and what future evidence would force a judgment to change.

Arion therefore organizes a cycle:

```text
reality
-> experience
-> counterfactuals
-> inquiry or bounded action
-> new reality
-> revised judgment
```

## Incompleteness

Every observation is partial. Missing evidence, conflicting signals, and
selection effects are conditions of judgment, not exceptional states.

A workflow may finish while important unknowns remain. Those unknowns must be
resolved, explicitly accepted with observation obligations, or left open. They
must never disappear because a report was produced.

## Asymmetry

False action and false inaction are rarely equivalent. Evidence requirements
should rise with potential loss, affected scope, irreversibility, and the
difficulty of detecting a mistake.

Arion records asymmetric consequences so that confidence is not mistaken for
the cost of being wrong.

## Revisability

A judgment is a bounded, accountable commitment under current conditions. It
is not an eternal truth.

Closing a judgment requires:

- an explicit statement;
- an explicit disposition of open unknowns and counterfactuals;
- at least one revision trigger;
- observation obligations for anything accepted as unresolved.

New experience can reopen a closed judgment. Revision is not a failure of the
system; it is how the system remains alive.

## Responsibility Conservation

Governance roles define durable responsibility and authority. Agent instances
are temporary executors for one bounded question.

Work can run in parallel, but accountability must remain identifiable.
Evidence constrains a judgment but does not own it. An Agent can execute a
task but cannot silently acquire the authority of its governance role.

## Problem-Driven Agents

The planner asks:

> Which irreducible questions remain unanswered?

It does not ask:

> Which role comes next in a standard sequence?

All open items at the highest active priority become assignments. Two
independent questions owned by one role become two parallel Agent instances.
One bounded assignment remains a Direct Run.

## Memory

Archived material is not automatically knowledge. Experience becomes durable
knowledge only when it can change a future prior, threshold, question,
boundary, or action—and when its source and revision conditions remain known.
